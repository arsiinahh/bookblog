About Us

Hi, I'm Dea — a reader, a dreamer, and someone who takes her tea with too much milk and her books with too many post-it notes.

This little corner of the internet is where I come to talk about the stories that moved me, the characters that felt like friends, and the books that made me want to call someone at midnight just to say "you have to read this."

Cozy Book Notes isn't about being a professional critic. It's about being honest — about the books I loved, the ones I didn't finish, and everything in between. If you find your next favorite read here, or just feel a little less alone in your reading life, then this page has done its job.
